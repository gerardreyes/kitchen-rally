Kitchen Rally

One-on-one pickleball against the CPU.

  • Rally scoring to 11, win by 2. Whoever wins a point serves next.
  • Serve diagonally. The serve and the return must bounce before anyone volleys.
  • No volleys inside the kitchen, the 7 ft zone either side of the net.
  • Hit from the middle of your paddle for accuracy. Stretching wide gets sloppy.
